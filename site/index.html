How it works

  1. Fetch. Pull every open role from 36 public Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever boards; keep titles with "product" or "program".
  2. Facts in code. Pay ranges, the remote flag and Toronto/Canada location fit come from regex. An early version asked the model for location and it labeled Vancouver roles as US-only, so location moved to code.
  3. Meaning from Jev. Six typed questions per posting (role kind, AI scope, seniority, ML required, hands-on AI skills required, engineering required), each with a probability. Buckets combine the answers in code; anything under 0.6 confidence goes to review.

Labels are model judgments. On 40 hand-labeled postings, Jev agreed with me on whether a role belongs in an AI bucket for 32 of 36 (4 went to review); full eval. Pay is what the posting lists; blank means not listed.

Postings come from each company's public careers board and link back to it. Built on the how-to-jev skill by Aakash Gupta (MIT).
